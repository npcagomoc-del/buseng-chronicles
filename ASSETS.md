## Generated game art and original effects

Restaurant, Rice Man, Buseng, Tahp and the rice-face icon were generated for this game using image generation with user-supplied reference images. The restaurant reference informed distant table proportions, amber atmosphere, materials and green/yellow palette; Mang Inasal logos are not reproduced. Character art remains photo-inspired, so this project does not purport to license a real person's likeness. Respect the people represented.

Rice grains, chicken-oil/fire, walking, gaze, sweat, teleport smear, plate slam and compact slap are original canvas effects. Music/fire cues are original procedural compositions. NBA Jam, Dragon Ball-style teleport, Donkey Kong-style plate-slam energy and spicy-noodle reactions inspired timing/feel, not copied franchise graphics/music. Third-party GIF frames are not bundled.

## Purpose

Hindi ginawa ang game para pagkakitaan ang characters. It is a free fan game for fun, learning and community improvement, inspired by Filipino meme culture, the recent viral Buseng video and Mang Inasal's unli-rice setting. No affiliation or sponsorship is implied.
